##### 5. The measures

All are **class-agnostic**, with A29's matching (cut-vIoU on option-B runs), for four systems on the
same videos: arm A, arm B, the 35-name reference and EOV-MMP R0w (G1's files). Ground truth comes from
A31's HELD-A extracts. Code: `studies/d7/check/d7_analyse.py` (`{ANALYSE_SHA}`).
- object recall, overall and base / novel;
- pair recall (not U1);
- burden (unmatched confident tracks per video);
- **recall at an equal track budget (reported, not gated):** per video, each system's confident tracks
  are cut to the reference's count of confident tracks. The highest A29 track scores are kept; ties go
  to the lower track id.
- **per pass:** seconds per frame, masklet count, peak GPU memory.

##### 6. The rule, and Giang's cap

**The rule (binding):**
- "The better arm" is the arm with the higher class-agnostic object-recall gain over R0w. Ties go to
  arm A.
- **D7 continues if that gain is at least half of the 35-name reference's gain** on the same videos.
- Burden, pair recall and equal-budget recall are reported, not gated.
- **If D7 stops:** PaD Phase 1 as designed stops. The fallbacks are those of the PaD proposal §9.

**Giang's cap (A33b §2):** D7 must fit 8 hours and $5, at the rented machine's price with its 200 GB
disk ($0.546/h).
- **Costing:** the arms at the slow end of their range, 0.25 s per frame-pass over 5 passes per frame
  of the final list. The reference rerun, if needed, at 35 passes per frame and check 0's own
  measured seconds per frame-prompt on this machine.
- **Before check 0:** if check 0 plus the arms exceed the cap, stop and ask.
- **After check 0:** if it is not bitwise and the rerun makes the total exceed the cap, stop and ask
  before the rerun. The machine waits.
